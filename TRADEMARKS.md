# AgentKeel Trademark Policy

The Apache-2.0 licence covers the **code**. It does not grant rights to the
**AgentKeel name and logo**. This policy keeps the name trustworthy: when users
see "AgentKeel", they should get the real project.

## You may, without asking

- Say your software "works with AgentKeel", "is compatible with AgentKeel" or
  "uses AgentKeel".
- Use the name and unmodified logo in articles, talks, tutorials and community
  events about AgentKeel.
- Name a plugin `agentkeel-plugin-<name>`, as long as it is clear it is a
  community plugin.
- Distribute unmodified official releases under the AgentKeel name.

## Please ask first

- Using the name or logo in a product name, company name or domain.
- Selling merchandise with the logo.
- Distributing modified versions under the AgentKeel name.

## Not allowed

- Implying official endorsement that does not exist.
- Using a confusingly similar name or logo for a competing project.
- Changing the logo's shape or colours beyond the variants in the brand kit.

Forks are welcome under a different name.

Questions: open a Discussion in the "Ideas" category or contact the lead
maintainer listed in MAINTAINERS.md.
